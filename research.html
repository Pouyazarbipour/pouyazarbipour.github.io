Research

Modeling coastal and ocean systems across reliability, data, and learning.

Research interests: coastal and ocean engineering; probabilistic and uncertainty quantification analyses; machine learning and deep learning applications; remote sensing; extreme events; causal inference; and hydrodynamics. Additional topics include time-dependent reliability of coastal structures, quantum machine learning, and physics-informed neural networks.

Reliability & uncertainty

Reliability-based design of berm breakwaters

Probabilistic and uncertainty-quantification analyses for reliability-informed design and risk assessment of coastal structures, including a new Reliability-Based Design Optimization (RBDO) approach for berm breakwaters with required performance and time-dependent reliability analysis of breakwaters.

  • Bayesian modification for reshaping berm breakwaters, with a case study of the northern coasts of the Oman Sea (Chabahar region).
  • Nature-based retrofit solutions for existing berm breakwaters under climate-change impacts (under review).
  • RBDO with different reshaping and dependency structures (in preparation).

Quantum & physics-informed ML

Wave overtopping: quantum ML, causal inference & Causal-PINN-UQ

Wave-overtopping estimation that integrates quantum machine learning (including a Quantum SVM) with causal inference and uncertainty quantification, and a causal-aware physics-informed neural surrogate with aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty. Related work develops a physics-informed neural operator with uncertainty quantification for spatial reconstruction of SWAN wave fields.

Remote sensing & causal deep learning

Harmful algal blooms and chlorophyll-a prediction

Deep-learning prediction of harmful algal blooms that bridges causality and neural networks, and spatiotemporal prediction of chlorophyll-a in semi-enclosed gulfs using a hybrid graph neural network–transformer framework driven by satellite data and causal analysis.

Desalination & marine environment

Probabilistic evaluation of desalination effluent discharge

M.Sc. thesis research at Tarbiat Modares University (excellent grade, 19.25/20; supervisor Dr. Hassan Akbari) on the probabilistic performance of effluent discharge from desalination plants, a reliability design of seawater desalination outfalls based on a novel probabilistic environmental assessment, and effluent-spread assessment for the Saqi Koothar Desalination Plant, Bandar Abbas.
